Military Service & Leadership

Luke Kay | Dustin Luke Kay | United States Navy

Before building a career in Talent Acquisition and recruiting leadership, Dustin Luke Kay — professionally known as Luke Kay — served in the United States Navy from 1996 to 2004.

His Navy experience helped establish a leadership foundation centered on accountability, career development, retention, service and supporting the people entrusted to his leadership.

VP-40

Sailor of the Quarter

While serving with VP-40 in 1999, Luke was selected as Sailor of the Quarter.

During this period, he served as an Associate Command Career Counselor and supported career development and retention efforts within a large maintenance department.

Military Recognition

Navy & Marine Corps Achievement Medal

Luke's military recognition included the Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal, recognizing performance during his United States Navy service.

The recognition represents part of a Navy career that combined technical responsibilities with career counseling, retention and leadership.

Leadership & Retention

Command Career Counseling

As an Associate Command Career Counselor, Luke worked with Sailors on career development and retention while supporting a maintenance organization of approximately 350 personnel.

The role required communication, trust, career guidance and the ability to connect individual career goals with the needs of the organization — skills that would later become central to Luke's civilian career in recruiting and Talent Acquisition leadership.

Military Recognition

Flag-Level Letters of Commendation

Luke's Navy service also included flag-level letters of commendation recognizing his military performance and service.

These recognitions became part of a broader record of leadership, professional achievement and service during his Navy career.

Humanitarian Service

1997 Truckee River Flood

Luke received the Humanitarian Service Medal associated with military response efforts following the 1997 Truckee River flood affecting the Reno, Sparks and Truckee Meadows area.

The experience represents an important part of Luke's military service and a commitment to helping communities during times of need.

Military & Community Recognition

U.S. Army Freedom Team Salute

Luke later received recognition through the U.S. Army Freedom Team Salute program following a nomination connected with his support of a service member.

From Military Service to Talent Acquisition

A Leadership Foundation That Continued Into Recruiting

The leadership principles developed during Luke's Navy service carried forward into a career spanning recruiting, technology, staffing, mortgage banking and Talent Acquisition leadership.

Career counseling in the Navy and recruiting in the private sector share an important principle: understanding people, identifying opportunity and helping individuals make meaningful career decisions.
