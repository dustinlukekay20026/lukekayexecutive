Executive Profile

About Luke Kay

Talent Acquisition Executive | Strategic Growth Leader | Mortgage Recruiting Specialist

Dustin Luke Kay, professionally known as Luke Kay, is a Talent Acquisition and recruiting leader whose career spans mortgage banking, technology, healthcare, corporate recruiting and staffing.

Over more than three decades in recruiting, Luke has worked across individual contributor, management and executive leadership positions, developing recruiting organizations, improving hiring performance and helping companies identify and attract high-impact talent.

Talent Acquisition Leadership

Building Recruiting Organizations That Produce Business Results

Luke's experience includes leadership and recruiting roles with organizations including PennyMac, American Pacific Mortgage, Accenture, Inogen, Academic Partnerships, JDA TSG and Metro Systems.

His work has included executive recruiting, mortgage sales recruiting, technical recruiting, healthcare recruiting, recruiting operations, sourcing strategy, recruiter development and Talent Acquisition organizational design.

Throughout his career, Luke has focused on connecting recruiting performance with measurable business outcomes rather than treating recruiting simply as a transactional hiring function.

Mortgage Banking

Mortgage Recruiting & Strategic Growth

Mortgage banking became one of the defining specialties of Luke Kay's recruiting career.

As Vice President of Talent Acquisition at PennyMac, Luke helped build a dedicated Loan Officer recruiting organization and significantly expanded annual Loan Officer hiring.

Later, as a Strategic Growth Partner with American Pacific Mortgage, he focused on recruiting and growth opportunities involving high-performing mortgage branches and Loan Officers across the United States.

His mortgage recruiting approach combines relationship development, market research, candidate engagement, production analysis and long-term recruiting strategy.
