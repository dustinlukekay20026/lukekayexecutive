Mortgage Recruiting & Strategic Growth

Luke Kay | Loan Officer Recruiting | Talent Acquisition Leadership

Dustin Luke Kay, professionally known as Luke Kay, is a Talent Acquisition executive with extensive experience recruiting Loan Officers, mortgage sales professionals and high-performing mortgage teams.

His mortgage recruiting career has combined executive search, relationship-driven recruiting, production analysis, recruiting operations and strategic growth initiatives designed to connect talent acquisition directly with business production.

PennyMac

Scaling Loan Officer Recruiting

As Vice President of Talent Acquisition at PennyMac, Luke helped build a dedicated Loan Officer Talent Acquisition organization designed to support large-scale mortgage sales growth.

During his first year, annual Loan Officer hiring increased from approximately 300 hires to approximately 900 hires — representing a 200% increase in annual hiring volume.

The expanded recruiting organization included seven dedicated Loan Officer recruiters and a structured recruiting process focused on candidate engagement, recruiter productivity and hiring velocity.

Average time-to-fill improved from approximately 36–42 days to approximately 28 days.

The additional Loan Officer hiring represented approximately $1.9 billion in additional annual loan production during the first year, based on production associated with the expanded hiring effort.

American Pacific Mortgage

Strategic Growth Recruiting

As a Strategic Growth Partner with American Pacific Mortgage, Luke focused on identifying and developing recruiting opportunities involving high-performing mortgage branches, Loan Officers and mortgage leaders.

Over a nine-month period, Luke conducted 209 targeted recruiting conversations that generated 94 opportunity meetings.

Seventy-nine of those meetings were completed, representing a strong meeting hold rate and demonstrating the effectiveness of a highly targeted, relationship-based recruiting strategy.

The recruiting opportunities represented more than $1.08 billion in potential annual loan production.

Luke ranked as the organization's leading recruiter for multiple consecutive months and was recognized as an Employee of the Year nominee.

Recruiting Strategy

Recruiting Beyond the Résumé

Mortgage recruiting requires more than identifying candidates who hold the correct title. Successful recruiting requires understanding production, market relationships, leadership structure, candidate motivations and the business opportunity behind every conversation.

Luke's recruiting methodology emphasizes targeted research, personalized outreach, long-term relationship development and a clear understanding of the business case associated with each recruiting opportunity.

This approach is particularly important when recruiting experienced Loan Officers and mortgage leaders who may already be successful with their current organizations.

Leadership

Building High-Performance Mortgage Recruiting Teams

Luke's mortgage recruiting experience includes both individual production and recruiting leadership.

His approach to Talent Acquisition leadership emphasizes measurable recruiter activity, meaningful candidate conversations, recruiter accountability, hiring velocity and business outcomes.

Rather than measuring recruiting success solely by activity volume, Luke focuses on the progression from candidate identification to conversation, opportunity meeting, hire and ultimately business impact.
