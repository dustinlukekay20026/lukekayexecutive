Executive Profile

Leadership at scale. Production at the individual level.

Luke Kay has built and led recruiting teams, supported complex enterprise organizations and personally recruited high-value passive talent. His career spans mortgage banking, technology consulting, healthcare, staffing and military leadership.

His differentiator is range: he can build the recruiting organization, establish metrics and process, partner with executive leadership — and still personally create conversations with difficult-to-reach producers and technical talent.
