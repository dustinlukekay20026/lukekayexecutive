Awards & Recognition

Luke Kay | Recruiting Leadership | Military Service | Career Achievement

Throughout more than three decades in recruiting, Talent Acquisition leadership and military service, Dustin Luke Kay — professionally known as Luke Kay — has received recognition for recruiting performance, leadership, service and professional achievement.

The recognition represented throughout this executive portfolio spans corporate recruiting, technology, staffing, mortgage banking and service in the United States Navy.

Recruiting Recognition

Top Recruiter of the Year

Luke earned Top Recruiter of the Year recognition during his recruiting career, reflecting sustained recruiting production and performance.

The recognition followed a track record of recruiter productivity, candidate engagement and successful delivery across demanding recruiting environments.

Recruiting Performance

Territory King Recognition

Earlier in his recruiting career, Luke received Territory King recognition for recruiting performance.

The award represents another stage of a career built around measurable recruiting production, relationship development and competitive performance.

Accenture

Top Recruiter Spotlight

While working as a Senior Technical Recruiter with Accenture, Luke was recognized through the company's Top Recruiter Spotlight.

During his first two quarters in 2015, Luke completed 172 hires, including 26 hires during a single month.

The recognition reflects high-volume recruiting delivery within a large-scale technology and professional services environment.

Metro Systems

Recruiting & Leadership Recognition

Luke's career with Metro Systems included recognition as Top Recruiter of the Year as well as National Recruiting Manager Champion.

His leadership responsibilities eventually included oversight of an organization of approximately 47 recruiters, combining individual recruiting performance with recruiting-team leadership.

American Pacific Mortgage

Strategic Growth Performance

As a Strategic Growth Partner with American Pacific Mortgage, Luke ranked as the organization's leading recruiter for multiple consecutive months.

His performance included 209 targeted recruiting conversations over a nine-month period, generating 94 opportunity meetings and identifying more than $1.08 billion in potential annual loan production.

Luke was also recognized as an Employee of the Year nominee.

United States Navy

Navy & Marine Corps Achievement Medal

During his United States Navy service, Luke received the Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal.

His military recognition also included Sailor of the Quarter while serving with VP-40 in 1999.

The Sailor of the Quarter recognition came while Luke served as an Associate Command Career Counselor supporting career development and retention within a large maintenance department.

Military Recognition

Flag-Level Letters of Commendation

Luke's Navy service included flag-level recognition and letters of commendation associated with his military performance and service.

His military career provided an early leadership foundation centered on accountability, career development, retention and service to others.

Humanitarian Service

Humanitarian Service Medal

Luke received the Humanitarian Service Medal associated with military response efforts following the 1997 Truckee River flood affecting the Reno, Sparks and Truckee Meadows area.

Service and community involvement would remain recurring themes throughout his military and civilian career.

Military & Community Recognition

U.S. Army Freedom Team Salute

Luke also received recognition through the U.S. Army Freedom Team Salute program following a nomination connected with his support of a service member.

This recognition reflects a broader pattern of leadership and support extending beyond traditional recruiting responsibilities.
