Talent Acquisition Leadership

Building Recruiting Organizations That Deliver Results

Luke Kay | Executive Recruiting | Talent Acquisition Strategy

Dustin Luke Kay, professionally known as Luke Kay, is a Talent Acquisition executive and recruiting leader whose career spans mortgage banking, technology, healthcare, staffing and corporate recruiting.

Over more than three decades in recruiting, Luke has worked across individual contributor, recruiting management and executive leadership positions while helping organizations attract talent, develop recruiting teams and improve hiring performance.

Leadership Philosophy

Connecting Talent Acquisition With Business Performance

Luke's approach to Talent Acquisition centers on the principle that recruiting should produce measurable business outcomes.

Successful recruiting organizations require more than candidate submissions and activity metrics. They require disciplined sourcing, meaningful candidate conversations, recruiter accountability, hiring-manager partnerships, measurable conversion and a clear understanding of the business being supported.

Throughout his career, Luke has focused on building recruiting strategies that connect people, process and performance.

PennyMac

Vice President, Talent Acquisition

At PennyMac, Luke served in Talent Acquisition leadership with a major focus on mortgage sales recruiting and Loan Officer growth.

He helped build a dedicated Loan Officer recruiting organization that expanded annual hiring from approximately 300 Loan Officers to approximately 900 during the first year.

The organization included seven dedicated Loan Officer recruiters, while average time-to-fill improved from approximately 36–42 days to approximately 28 days.

The expanded hiring effort represented approximately $1.9 billion in additional annual loan production during the first year.

Accenture

Senior Technical Recruiting

Luke's technology recruiting background includes experience as a Senior Technical Recruiter with Accenture.

During his first two quarters in 2015, Luke completed 172 hires, including 26 hires during a single month.

His recruiting performance resulted in recognition through Accenture's Top Recruiter Spotlight.

The role strengthened a career foundation in high-volume technical recruiting, candidate engagement and recruiting delivery within a large-scale professional services environment.

Inogen

Talent Acquisition Leadership Across Corporate Functions

At Inogen, Luke's Talent Acquisition leadership responsibilities supported a recruiting organization managing approximately 78 open positions.

The recruiting team included three recruiters and two sourcers and supported functions including research and development, engineering, finance, medical, human resources and information technology.

This environment required balancing specialized recruiting needs with broader organizational hiring priorities.

JDA TSG

Director of Talent Acquisition

As Director of Talent Acquisition with JDA TSG, Luke supported highly specialized technology recruiting requirements and client delivery.

His work included recruiting connected with Microsoft professional services environments and clients including Intuit and the NFL.

The position required identifying specialized technical talent while developing recruiting solutions for demanding client environments.

Academic Partnerships

Building Internal Recruiting Capability

At Academic Partnerships, Luke worked in senior recruiting while helping support the transition from an agency-dependent recruiting approach toward a stronger internal Talent Acquisition model.

His responsibilities included national recruiting and the development of recruiting practices designed to improve internal hiring capability.

Metro Systems

Regional Recruiting Leadership

Luke's leadership experience at Metro Systems included responsibility for a recruiting organization of approximately 47 recruiters.

His performance resulted in recognition including Top Recruiter of the Year and National Recruiting Manager Champion.

The experience provided an early foundation for leading recruiters, developing recruiting performance and managing Talent Acquisition at scale.

American Pacific Mortgage

Strategic Growth Through Recruiting

As a Strategic Growth Partner with American Pacific Mortgage, Luke applied executive recruiting principles to mortgage growth opportunities.

During a nine-month period, 209 targeted recruiting conversations generated 94 opportunity meetings, with 79 meetings completed.

The identified recruiting opportunities represented more than $1.08 billion in potential annual loan production.

Luke ranked as the organization's leading recruiter for multiple consecutive months and was recognized as an Employee of the Year nominee.
